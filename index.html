03Services

Send the footage. Get back a finished video.

One editor for the whole finish, so the story, design and sound all feel like the same video.

01

Story & pacing

I find the strongest line, cut the dead air and keep the rhythm calm. Nothing jumpy for the sake of it.

02

Captions that read

Nepali, Romanised Nepali or English, in your brand fonts and placed where the viewer is already looking.

03

Motion graphics

Product cards, diagrams, lower thirds and end cards that explain the idea without crowding the frame.

04

Sound design & mix

Clean dialogue, steady loudness on every platform, and effects that support the cut instead of distracting from it.

05

Colour

Shots matched to each other while keeping your camera’s natural look. No heavy filters.

06

Delivery

Vertical, square or 16:9, up to 4K, plus project files so your captions stay editable later.

04Process

How a project runs

  1. Marker 1

    Brief

    Tell me the platform, the audience and the deadline. Share a video you like and I’ll match its style.

  2. Marker 2

    First cut

    Story and pacing first, so we agree on the structure before any polish goes on.

  3. Marker 3

    Revisions

    Each round is a new numbered version, so nothing you approved ever gets lost.

  4. Marker 4

    Delivery

    Final files for every platform, ready to post on the day you need them.
